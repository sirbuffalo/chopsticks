Rules

Tap one of the bot's hands with one of yours. The bot's hand increases by your hand's value.

Hands wrap at 5, so 5 becomes 0 and that hand is out.

You win by making both bot hands 0.

Instead of tapping, you can rearrange your own hands as long as the total stays the same and the result is a real change.

A position cannot appear for the third time. If no legal move remains, the game is a draw.

Keyboard: Tab or arrow keys move focus. Enter or Space selects.
